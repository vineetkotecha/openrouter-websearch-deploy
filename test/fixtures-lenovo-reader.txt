## Features

## Enhanced Performance

With Intel® Core™ Ultra processors, the ThinkPad E16 Gen 2 (16″ Intel) laptop brings AI-assisted productivity to the forefront. Enjoy optimized performance with dedicated AI engines, including the low-power neural processing unit (NPU) that enables you to do much more on battery power. Multitask away, even when unplugged — turn on auto-framing, background blur, and low-light enhancement for your online meetings.

![Image 35](https://p1-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/zpuzenaiwgirevtfhwakefypg46ff5766229.jpg)

## Accurate & Ergonomic Keyboard

The ThinkPad E16 G2 (16″ Intel) laptop features a full-sized keyboard that includes a numeric pad — perfect for financial services, data analysis, or any detailed tasks involving number crunching. Navigate with the TrackPad or the TrackPoint and leave your mouse behind. Plus, with a double tap of the little red nub, the ThinkPad TrackPoint Quick Menu opens to optimize audio-visuals, set alerts against muting, and even take notes with the Dictation Toolbar for handy speech-to-text transcription.

![Image 36](https://p3-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/hmchq009o7oiq7y9n3rcz65enzii5f261398.jpg)

## Sound Collaborator

The laptop boasts an impressive display, offering an exceptional visual experience. With a range of sharp and focused resolutions available, images are rendered with stunning detail and vibrancy. Keep your meetings in focus and collaboration to a maximum. This ThinkPad is ideal for anyone on the go or working from home. In addition, this laptop boasts Audio by Harman® and Dolby Atmos® that’ll help you rock out to your favorite media and sound crystal-clear during conference calls.

![Image 37](https://p4-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/ose27wvjlhhmbz3wmihiuhyx8i4iyz052764.jpg)

## Ready to Work, Anywhere

We use the US Department of Defense's MIL-STD 810H to create a balance of reliability and durability for all of our ThinkPad laptops. Meeting or exceeding 12 standards, 26 procedures, and 200+ quality checks ensure that these devices run in extreme conditions, including harsh variables like Arctic wilderness, desert dust storms, extreme temps, pressure, humidity, vibration, and more.

![Image 38](https://p4-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/o1nwioxkp9cghdf937kdkqx62tgxey692759.jpg)

## Recycled Components & Eco-conscious Packaging

Even with all its productivity and collaboration-enhancing features, this ThinkPad was designed with recycling in mind.We used recycled post-consumer content (PCC) in various components. The battery pack is made of 25% PCC recycled plastic, which is a big step towards sustainability. The speaker enclosure is 30% PCC recycled plastic, while the AC Adaptor rocks a cool 90% PCC recycled plastic composition. Plus, the packaging is completely plastic-free and 100% recyclable – and is Forest Stewardship Council® certified, so you can feel good about choosing this laptop.

![Image 39](https://p4-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/on5uo5zdedk2tt76ht34jfbtbm3jnz054268.jpg)

## Safe & Secure Computing

Biometrics provide extra protection on the ThinkPad E16 Gen 2 (16″ Intel) laptop — from the fingerprint reader integrated with the power button to the facial recognition software that works with the IR camera. Our robust suite of security solutions, ThinkShield, includes discrete Trusted Platform Module (dTPM) 2.0 that ensures the encryption of vital data. Moreover, it uses AI-enabled Intel® Threat Detection Technology to proactively guard against threats such as ransomware and cryptojacking. With ThinkPad, rest assured, that your security and asset protection are our utmost priority.

![Image 40](https://p2-ofp.static.pub//fes/cms/2024/03/07/je2xgsmfqftfuqtflxe32isyg9ypju162468.jpg)
